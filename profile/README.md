## First Look at AlbumPlayer

AlbumPlayer is for people who keep album folders they own and want one named album desk, not mixed untitled cue sheets. It gives you own albums, a cover desk, and a short path back to a cue sheet you already keep. It does not replace the rest of your PC. It sits beside the discs you already use.

Work stays on albums you own. Extra folders wait for their own title.
